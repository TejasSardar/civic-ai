# Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan (PM-USP)

## Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS)


## Category

Higher Education Scholarship


## Government

Government of India

Ministry of Education

Department of Higher Education


## Objective

The scheme provides financial assistance to meritorious students
from low-income families to help meet part of their day-to-day
expenses while pursuing higher studies.


## Eligibility

For fresh applications, the official 2025-26 FAQ states that
students must meet the following conditions:

- Be above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in the
  relevant stream from their particular Class XII examination board.

- Be pursuing a regular course.

- Correspondence and distance-mode courses are not eligible.

- Have annual family income up to Rs. 4.5 lakh.

- Not be receiving another scholarship or fee reimbursement
  of any kind.

- Diploma students are not eligible.

- Students who took a drop after Class XII are not eligible.


## Important

Meeting the conditions above does not by itself guarantee
selection.

Beneficiaries are selected through the scheme's merit and
allocation process.


## Benefit

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible students
pursuing higher studies.


## Application

The scheme is implemented through the National Scholarship Portal.
